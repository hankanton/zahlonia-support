Help & support

Tallonia (called Zahlonia in German) is a role-playing game for practising maths. Count Scramble has put a spell on the land: in the Number Tower, number creatures wait to be defeated with correctly solved sums. Along the way you free the villagers, build your own home, rescue animals and explore the Number Forest and the Vaults. The sums follow the curriculum of the chosen region and grade and adapt to how your child is doing.

The first three floors of the Tower, the village and all the maths practice are free – no ads and no time limit. A one-time purchase unlocks the full version.

Frequently asked questions

I can't hear any sound.

Tallonia plays sound even in silent mode, like music and video apps. Check the device volume (volume buttons) and the sliders for music, sounds and voices under Settings in the game. To make the game quieter or mute it, use the volume buttons or the settings. If it still stays silent, please tell us which device and iOS version you are using.

How do I get into the parents' area?

Tap “For parents” in the settings. A number is shown as a word; type it in as digits. You can also set your own parent PIN.

How do I set the region, school type and grade?

In the parents' area under “Play mode & grade”. The curricula of all German federal states, Austria, the German-speaking and bilingual Swiss cantons, Liechtenstein and South Tyrol are included (the game is in English, the curricula are those of these regions). If you follow a different curriculum, choose “By Level” instead. Under “General”, “We're currently on…” lets you say which topic is being covered at school right now; that topic then comes up more often.

The sums are too hard or too easy.

The game adapts to your child's progress: what is still shaky comes up more often, and after a mistake the same kind of sum soon returns with new numbers. If your child is ahead or wants to revise, choose “By Level” instead of “By My School” in the parents' area under “Play mode & grade”. Under “General” you can also set how strong the creatures are; this does not change the sums themselves. “Progress” shows which topics are already secure.

What is free, and what does the full version add?

Floors 1 to 3 of the Tower, the village and the maths for every grade are free, with no ads and no time limit. The full version unlocks Floors 4 to 10, the Number Forest, the Vaults, all animals and the upgrades for the village and your home. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It can only be bought in the parents' area under “Full version”; there is no buy button in the game itself. The app shows the current price there, straight from the App Store.

The full version is gone after reinstalling.

In the parents' area under “Full version”, tap “Restore purchases”. The device must be signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Usually the full version also comes back by itself when the app starts. On Android the same applies, with the Google account that was used to buy in the Play Store.

Does the purchase cover the whole family?

Yes. The full version applies to all players on the device and, through Apple's Family Sharing, on your family's devices too. If “Ask to Buy” is turned on for a child's account, the purchase waits until a parent approves it; after that the full version appears by itself. On Android, the full version applies to all players on the device and on other Android devices with the same Google account; Google Play Family Library does not share in-app purchases. If a purchase needs approval in Family Link, it waits in the same way until approved.

I've received a code.

In the parents' area under “Full version”, tap “Redeem code”. Alternatively, tap your profile picture in the App Store and choose “Redeem Gift Card or Code”. On Android, “Redeem code” opens the Play Store; alternatively, tap your profile picture there and choose “Payments & subscriptions” › “Redeem gift code”.

New device or reinstalled the app: how do I get our games back?

Tallonia backs up the games to iCloud, in the Apple Account signed in on the device, if iCloud is turned on there. The new device must be signed in with the same Apple Account. On first launch on the new device, the app asks “Saved games found in iCloud”; tap “Restore” to continue from the last backup. Later you can do this in the parents' area under “General” › “Backup” › “Restore from iCloud…”. The backup is not a live sync: if you play on two devices in turn, each keeps its own games, and iCloud always holds the backup of the device that backed up last. On Android, Android itself does the backup: about once a day, encrypted, in the Google account on the device, if device backup is on and the device has a screen lock. If a new device is set up from this backup or Tallonia is reinstalled, the games are back by themselves – without asking.

I'm stuck at the Golem.

With gold and ore you can improve your weapon and armour at the Forge, and the Herb Shop sells potions. Both make the fight easier. Solving lots of sums correctly also makes you stronger over time.

Can several children play on one device?

Yes. Up to six children each get their own profile with their own grade, and each child can have up to three games with their own hero. You manage this in the parents' area under “Players”.

Can I limit play time?

Yes, in the parents' area under “Play time”: a limit per day, optionally different at weekends. When time is up, the game ends in a friendly way and saves. You can extend the time for one day.

Is the game saved?

Yes, continuously on the device and also as a backup in iCloud, in the Apple Account on the device (can be turned off in the parents' area under “General” › “Backup”; “Delete iCloud backup…” there removes an existing copy). You can stop at any time with “Take a break” in the menu; next time it carries on from the same spot. On Android, instead of iCloud, in the device backup of the Google account (switch “Backup to Google account” in the same place).

How do I reset a game or delete a profile?

In the parents' area: “General” › “Reset game…”, and a child together with their games under “Players”. For safety, deleting has to be held down for three seconds.

App version: in the parents' area under “Help & licences”.
