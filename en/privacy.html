Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. The German version is legally binding.

In short: Tallonia needs no account, collects no personal data and shows no ads. Profiles, saved games, progress and settings are stored on your device. In addition, there is a backup copy of the games – on iPhone and iPad in iCloud, in the Apple Account on the device, on Android in the device backup of the Google account (each can be turned off) – and you buy the full version through Apple's App Store or through Google Play. We have no servers of our own and no access to any of this data.

2. What data the app processes

2.1 Data on the device

The app stores the following locally on the device: the children's profile names (freely chosen, e.g. a first name or nickname), the hero's name, chosen pictures, region, school type and grade, saved games, progress (which sums were solved correctly or incorrectly), play time, settings, a parent PIN if one was set, and a “letter to your child” if one was written. This data is not sent to us or to third parties. It can be reset or deleted in the parents' area and is removed completely when the app is deleted.

2.2 Error log

To help find bugs, the app writes a technical log (e.g. crash reports) on the device. It contains no names or learning results, stays on the device and is not sent automatically. Parents can view it in the parents' area under “Help & licences”.

2.3 iCloud backup

If iCloud is turned on on the device, Tallonia stores a copy of the profiles and saved games in the iCloud key-value storage of the Apple Account signed in on the device. The copy contains the game data listed under 2.1, but not the parent PIN. It is used only to restore the games after reinstalling or on a new device. It is stored and transferred by Apple; we have no access to it. The backup is on from the start and can be turned off in the parents' area under “General” › “Backup”. After that nothing more is backed up. An existing copy can be removed in the same place with “Delete iCloud backup…”. Apple's processing is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

2.4 Buying the full version

The first floors are free; the full version is a one-time in-app purchase. Purchase and payment are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The app receives neither payment details nor information about your Apple Account. It only asks Apple whether the full version has been bought or unlocked through Family Sharing or a code, and remembers this on the device. From Apple we only receive aggregated sales figures that do not identify anyone. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase.

2.5 Android: backup to the Google account and buying through Google Play

On Android devices, the following applies instead of 2.3 and 2.4: Android itself backs up the games with the device backup in the Google account signed in on the device. For this, Tallonia releases only the profiles and saved games (the game data listed under 2.1, but not the parent PIN), and only if the backup is end-to-end encrypted (device with a screen lock). It is stored and transferred by Google; we have no access to it. The backup is on from the start and can be turned off in the parents' area under “General” › “Backup” with “Backup to Google account”; Android then replaces the existing copy with the next backup. Purchase and payment of the full version are handled entirely by Google Play. The app receives neither payment details nor information about your Google account. It only asks Google Play whether the full version has been bought or unlocked with a code, and remembers this on the device. We use order data that Google provides to us for billing and refunds only for those purposes. Google's processing is covered by Google's privacy policy.

2.6 No accounts, no ads, no analytics

The app requires no registration, contains no ads, uses no analytics or tracking services and does not access the camera, microphone, location or contacts. Apart from the backup and the purchase through Apple or Google, it makes no connections to the internet.

4. Children

The app is aimed at children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Profile names and progress are kept only on the device and in its backup (iCloud or Google account) and are never sent to us. The parents' area is protected by a task for adults and, optionally, a PIN.

5. Legal basis

Where personal data is processed at all, this is done to provide the app, its backup and purchase, and these pages (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Because the app data is stored only on your device and in your iCloud or Google account, we hold no data about you that we could hand over or delete. On the device you can view and delete it in the parents' area. If you have questions, write to hankanton.apps@gmx.de.

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is always available at this address.
